01 · Overview Proposed

A Tuesday table that happens to be a website.

General Trivia is a quiz club on GitHub Pages: study or play, then come back. The General smiles with a beer. The hit counter still works. The questions are the point.

Jovial
The mark is laughing. Copy can wink; stems stay fair.
Clubhouse
Paper, gold, serif display — a room, not a dashboard.
Webmaster-sincere
Badges and guestbooks are garnish, not a bit that eats the quiz.
Brisk
Start quiz is ink. Get to the question.
Specific
Nights have dates. A new briefing is a real stamp.

04 · Typography Proposed

Fraunces for the room. Source Sans for the facts.

Both licensed on Google Fonts (OFL). Flip Display in the sidebar to try Source Sans on headings — researched alternate, not the default.

05 · Iconography Proposed

Custom chrome. Pixel badges. No Lucide.

Home is a 24px fill glyph. Webmaster badges are 132×47 and stay at the bottom of home.

06 · Photography & illustration Proposed

Comic General. Editorial posters. Diagram maps.

Do not teal-grade a Netflix still. Do not photograph a stand-in for the General. Maps stay in data/geography/maps/.

07 · Mascots Proposed

The General greets. He does not ask the questions.

Unnamed in UI copy. Three stars, mustache, beer. The Win95 illegal-operation dialog is a Konami egg — not a second character.

Do

  • Home header badge
  • This book’s cover
  • Favicon derivatives of the same drawing

Don’t

  • Put him on every category card
  • Generate a “younger general”
  • Use him in error states except the Konami gag

09 · Shadows & borders Proposed

Soft cards. Hard badges.

Cards float in paper air. Webmaster badges get a 1px/2px offset like 1998 meant it.

10 · Motion Proposed

Unhurried garnish. Snappy controls.

160–180ms on buttons and cards. The ticker takes 40s to lap. The eye glint takes 8.6s. Reduced motion stops all of it.

12 · Voice Proposed

Friendly webmaster. Fair quizmaster.

Warm, specific, PG. Can clutch pearls in the guestbook. Does not gamify, unlock, or crush it.

13 · AI policy Proposed

AI-assisted.

Photo parsing on the upload tool may use a vision model. Question banks are human-edited JSON. Do not generate a new General.
